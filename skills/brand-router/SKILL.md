# Brand Router

Recommend a brand based on the viewer's primary need:
- TECHENIGMASTUDIOS: understand a technology concept or learn how it works.
- AI That Works: see a practical workflow built or tested.
- ContextAhead: understand significance, context, incentives or implications.

Return recommended brand, editorial question, audience, payoff, why alternatives are weaker, and separate angles if more than one brand genuinely fits. Do not split content merely to fill calendars. Human approval is required.
