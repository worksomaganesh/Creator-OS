# Topic Researcher

Turn a topic signal into a traceable research brief.

Inputs: audience question, intended brand, audience, time sensitivity.

Process: restate the question; identify demand evidence; gather primary and credible independent sources; separate demand signals from factual evidence; identify coverage gaps; list claims, sources and uncertainties; recommend proceed, revise, monitor or reject.

Output: `research/research-brief-template.md`.

Never fabricate sources, search volumes, quotes or findings. State when research is incomplete.
