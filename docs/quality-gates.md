# Quality Gates

Publication is blocked until all critical checks pass.

| Gate | Questions | Blocking examples |
|---|---|---|
| Audience | Who is this for? What value will they receive? | No clear audience or payoff |
| Brand | Does this fit this brand's editorial role? | Duplicate or misrouted concept |
| Evidence | Are material claims supported? | Fabricated or untraceable claims |
| Editorial | Is the narrative clear and honest? | Misleading hook or unsupported conclusion |
| Production | Do visuals serve the story? | Incorrect diagram or unreadable text |
| Rights/privacy | Are assets permitted and data safe? | Unlicensed media or exposed private data |
| Accessibility | Are captions, contrast, and readability adequate? | Missing essential captions or illegible text |
| Technical | Is the export correct? | Broken audio, wrong aspect ratio, corrupted file |
| Human approval | Has a person reviewed the final deliverable? | No final approval |

## Review severity

- **Blocker:** must be fixed before publication.
- **Major:** materially harms accuracy, clarity, rights, or viewer experience.
- **Minor:** polish improvement; may be deferred with a recorded reason.

AI-generated review is advisory. Final approval remains human.
