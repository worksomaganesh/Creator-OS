# Content Opportunity Evaluation

Use this as a decision aid, not an automatic publishing score. Rate each dimension from 1 (weak) to 5 (strong).

| Dimension | What to assess |
|---|---|
| Audience relevance | Does the target audience care about this question? |
| Evidence of demand | Are there repeated questions, search signals, or observed needs? |
| Differentiation | Can this brand add a distinct explanation, demonstration, or context? |
| Evidence quality | Are trustworthy sources or reproducible tests available? |
| Timeliness | Is there a meaningful reason to publish now? |
| Evergreen value | Will the content remain useful after the current moment? |
| Production feasibility | Can we make it well with available time and tools? |
| Brand fit | Does it clearly belong to this brand? |

Suggested total: 40 points. Use scores to compare candidates, but do not treat a threshold as permission to publish. A weak evidence score or unclear brand fit may be a hard stop regardless of total.

For each candidate, write:
- audience and question
- evidence supporting demand
- competing coverage and its gap
- proposed angle
- sources or test plan
- production estimate
- risks and uncertainty
- decision and reason
